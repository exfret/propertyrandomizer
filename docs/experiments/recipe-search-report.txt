Root cause and trace

recipe-ingredients.custom_prereq_search prepares each recipe's staged regional
budget and material prices. find_valid_prereq_list applies the existing promotion,
reachability, automatability, result/fuel/furnace, duplicate, and price checks.
The recipe handler retains the inherited first_pass=true declaration from
handlers/default.lua and continues sharing the runner's existing pass.
The handler then tries its existing paying-pool / shape-ladder searches and
reflects accepted ingredients through the unified runner. Failed searches retain
the existing vanilla fallback and promised-context checks. This extension point
already owns progression decisions; the optimization adds no graph build, sort,
validation pass, retry loop, node type, or alternative progression algorithm.

recipe-cost.search_for_ings selects an initial distinct ingredient set, fits its
amounts, and tries compatible one-slot substitutions. It checks the target only
after a candidate, and retains the existing 30-candidate stall / 0.5-point
progress rule. Each proposed set calls calculate_optimal_amounts, which initializes
partial budgets, ramps the allocation by 1/(3*ingredient_count), and samples
2*ingredient_count coordinate proposals per round before applying one winner.
optimize_single_ing brackets and trisects each amount using the same cost score.

Removing the discarded pool advances the shared sort RNG differently, changing
later matching, availability, staged prices, and candidate-search trajectories.
The amount fitter and ingredient handler themselves are byte-identical between
the historical baseline and the fresh combined baseline. The saved profiles
already ruled out more unified attempts or matching refinements as the cause.

The highest scrap/aggregate unit-price ratio among those six materials can supply
only 22.43 scrap cost at that aggregate total. Exact matching of both objectives
is impossible for that particular set. Similar observed maxima are 36.28 against
3,432.32 for the recorded fusion-reactor-equipment set, and 53.34 against
10,914.29 for captive-biter-spawner. This arithmetic is about measured optimizer
prices, not a claim about intrinsic game resource requirements or every member
of the full candidate pool. It explains why fitting has to trade off objectives
and why subsequent substitutions can remain far above the score threshold.

Historical searches stop at threshold/exhaustion in 258/12 cases, with 10 kept-only
and 14 failed initial selections. Current searches stop at threshold/stall/
exhaustion in 243/13/13 cases, with 8 kept-only and 20 failed initial selections.
The number of successful nonempty search calls returning a bonus-adjusted score
above 1.5 grows from 4/270 to 19/269. Aggregate drift alone obscures this change:
historical/current mean absolute log drift is 0.095/0.096, while vanilla fallbacks
rise from 1 to 4. The bonus-adjusted score includes the existing novelty and
non-starting-position bonuses; it is not a raw aggregate cost ratio.

Implemented improvement

1. Cache scores by amount only inside one optimize_single_ing invocation. The
   target and all other ingredient amounts are fixed in that scope. Bracketing,
   trisection, endpoint checks and terminal cases reuse exactly the same scores.
   No cache survives a changed coordinate vector, target, or recipe.
2. Fit each sampled ingredient index once within a round. All original RNG draws
   are consumed; every proposal sees the same vector until the round finishes.
   Preserve first-best tie handling and apply the winner only after those draws.
3. Reuse the scaled target once allocation stops changing at one. Keep every
   allocation step and stopping check. Remove temporary proposal-list records
   and per-proposal parameter tables. Scope the two accidental temporaries
   dont_preserve_resource_costs and middle_amount locally.

This removes arithmetic and allocation without weakening the search. It does
not attempt to change the established balancing design, suppress difficult
candidates, lower budgets, relax safety checks, or special-case any seed.

candidate attempts, accepted substitutions, and fit stopping reasons. The saved
longest-fit replay inputs, RNG states, chosen amounts, scores, and traces also
match exactly. Search calls stay at 297, amount fits at 2,625, rounds at 43,070,
and proposal RNG draws at 427,024. Actual coordinate optimizations fall from
437,919 to 194,763 (-55.5%); score evaluations from 7,325,368 to 2,344,735
(-68.0%); cost-vector evaluations from 7,333,683 to 2,353,050 (-67.9%).
The remaining 194,763 repeated amount-cost evaluations reconstruct the returned
best cost vector once per coordinate fit; cached scores do not retain all those
transient cost tables. See probe-comparison.json in the evidence directory.
