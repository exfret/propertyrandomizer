Cost-window retry investigation

move_identity_sources assumed every fluid production alternative had an orand wrapper; selective OR expansion leaves direct edges, which were silently skipped. Barrel emptying therefore incorrectly remained a source for the fluid position, although reflection keeps it with its fluid identity.

Earlier versus later experiment: all six source hashes recorded by the earlier window study match the saved earlier source. That source used full gutils.make_orands(spoofed_graph). The later tested source uses selective expansion (commit f72ed64), so unclaimable fluid production edges have no wrappers. The rewiring helper still required wrappers. Earlier zero-retry measurements and the later six-attempt result therefore ran different implementations of the model.

Concrete first rejected candidate:
  crude-oil position -> fluoroketone-cold identity (available early).
  fluoroketone-cold position -> thruster-fuel identity.
  Real barrels keep fluoroketone-cold; they do not turn it into thruster fuel.
  The model erroneously kept empty-fluoroketone-cold-barrel -> fluid-craft-temperature fluoroketone-cold on the POSITION.
  Its proof for express-underground-belt used early fluoroketone identity -> filled barrel -> empty barrel -> fluoroketone position -> the newly selected fluid ingredient.
  Reflection renamed that position ingredient to thruster-fuel. The real thruster-fuel source was fluoroketone-cooling, not emptying the fluoroketone barrel, and it was unreachable. Hundreds of downstream losses followed.

Correction: Rewire direct edges as well as wrapped alternatives, preserving edge metadata and updating branch bookkeeping only when a wrapper exists.
