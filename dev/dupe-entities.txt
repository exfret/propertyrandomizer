# Entities whose duplicates get recolored graphics (dev/make-dupe-graphics.py): where the paint is, and the dupes' colors
#   name hue=H[,H2] dupes=C2,C3 [mask parameters]
#       The paint: pixels within half degrees (25) of a listed hue. Pixels with saturation >= sat (0.35) and
#       brightness >= val (0.3) surely are paint and seed the mask; it grows through connected pixels of the hue with
#       saturation >= low (half of sat) down to dark shadow; then it is filled by panel: where at least dens (0.5) of
#       a win x win window (9) is already paint, the highlights and weathering in between are too, a closing of radius
#       close (2) bridges gaps, regions under min pixels (40) go and holes under hole pixels (800) fill.
#       dupes= gives the first dupes' colors as hues (0 red, 30 orange, 50 yellow, 130 green, 185 teal), each optionally
#       with its own saturation as H/S and brightness as H/S/B (under 1 darker, over 1 paler). Every line gets dupes 2 to 9
#       (the setting propertyrandomizer-dupe-count picks how many a game uses): past its own list, the colors are filled
#       from the generator's palette (MUTED_PALETTE: these five, lime, then their darker and paler shades; no blue or
#       purple), skipping hues near the paint's or an avoid= hue and colors too close to the paint or an earlier dupe.
#       avoid=H[,H2] keeps the filled colors away from those hues (or white or black): the colors a relative wears.
#       strength= (0.7) is how far a masked pixel goes to the dupe color: lower it where the mask has to be large.
#       Check a line with: dev/make-dupe-graphics.py --dump ... --inspect DIR --only NAME
#   name grey dupes=C2,C3 [val=V] [smax=S] [vmax=X] [mask parameters]
#       For a machine with no paint: its light metal plates are the mask (saturation <= smax (0.18), brightness
#       between val (0.3) and vmax (0.97) seed it, it grows into slightly duller or darker plate), so rust and shadow stay
#   name whole dupes=C2,C3
#       the whole sprite is colorized: only a fallback
# Colors avoid what a relative already wears: no red or blue inserter (long-handed, fast; and no yellow one, the plain
