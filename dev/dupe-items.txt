# Items whose duplicates get recolored graphics (dev/make-dupe-graphics.py), in the line format of dev/dupe-entities.txt:
#   name hue=H[,H2] dupes=C2,C3 [mask parameters]      paint of the given hue is the mask
#   name grey dupes=C2,C3 [val=V] [smax=S] [...]       the light unsaturated parts are the mask
#   name whole dupes=C2,C3                              the whole sprite, only a fallback
# dupes= gives the first dupes' colors (H, H/S or H/S/B); the rest up to dupe 9 are filled from the generator's palette
# as in dev/dupe-entities.txt, away from the paint's and any avoid= hues (palette=vivid: the strong colors all around the
# wheel instead of the muted ones). What gets recolored: the item's icon and
# belt pictures, the icons of the recipes that make it, the grid sprite of the equipment it places and, for an armor, the
# character's animation sheets for it (lib/dupe.lua copies the animations for the armor's dupe).
# Check a line with: dev/make-dupe-graphics.py --dump ... --inspect DIR --only NAME
#

# Science packs: copied only with their planet's copy (lib/dupe-planets.lua), each locked to that copy. The flask's liquid is
# the paint. A pack copy has no number badge (user, 2026-09-30), so its color alone has to tell it apart: no copy shares a
# color with a pack of the same flask shape (base packs are conical: red 0, gold 42, green 122, cyan 196, violet 277, grey,
# white; Space Age's round: orange 27, chartreuse 67, blue 236, navy 244, magenta 330), and the copies all differ. Only the
# hue 160 is free of every pack, so the other copies share a hue with a pack of the other shape, told apart by the flask
# (and by lightness where the source is dark: brown from military, maroon from cryogenic). The old utility copy (185) read
# as a chemical pack. Each color carries a strong saturation (the /S in dupes=), like the originals.
# The second color is the third copy's (dupe 3; the original is 1). With 26 packs only lime and teal were free among the
# conical flasks, so utility's third copy is bright orange (no other conical pack is orange; military's brown copy is much
# darker) and military's is lime-olive (the closest call, next to logistic's green). Each round third copy takes a hue a
# conical original has (red: automation, gold: utility, cyan: chemical, dark green: logistic), told apart by flask shape and
# lightness. Runners-up for a slot: pale mint 140/0.35, pale lavender 270/0.3
# split=side (user, 2026-09-30): each copy colors only the right half of the liquid, so the left half keeps the original's
# color and a copy reads as its original's color beside its own
# Copies 4 to 9 (the fourth to ninth planet copies) are filled from the vivid palette: only kept apart from their own pack's
# color and its other copies, since with up to 56 copies of 7 packs no color stays free of every other pack; the left half
# still tells whose copy it is
